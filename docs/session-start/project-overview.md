# Project Overview

> What is El Woods and what are we building?

---

## The Business: El Woods

**El Woods** is a planned immersive sustainable retail + cafe experience in West Denver, Colorado.

### Tagline
> "Fuck normal, I want magic."

### The Vision

El Woods transforms sustainable shopping from a transaction into a **transformative experience**. Customers step into a woodland sanctuary featuring:

- Living walls and abundant greenery
- Reclaimed wood fixtures
- Natural lighting and organic textures
- Forest-inspired atmosphere

### Three Integrated Components

#### 1. Wardrobe Revival (Clothing) - 65% of Revenue
- Buy-sell-trade curated fashion
- Vintage, repurposed, eco-conscious pieces
- Price range: $15-$100+
- 60-70% gross margins
- Highly selective curation (20-30% acceptance rate)

#### 2. Eco Cafe - 30% of Revenue
- Locally sourced coffee and tea ($5-$7)
- Baked goods from local bakeries ($3-$6)
- Compostable packaging, zero-waste practices

#### 3. Collective - 5% of Revenue
- Community events and workshops
- Rotating local artist gallery
- Open mic nights, book swaps
- Consignment for sustainable designers

## The Mission

### Environmental Impact (Year 2+)
- **5-10% of net proceeds** donated to environmental causes
- Focus: Wildfire prevention, deforestation awareness
- Year 2 donation projection: $10,000+
- Year 3 donation projection: $20,000+

### Operational Sustainability (Year 1+)
- Clothing diverted from landfill: 3,000+ items/year
- Zero-waste cafe operations
- Compliance with Denver's Waste No More ordinance
- Impact is built into every transaction

### Community Impact
- Platform for local artists (70-80% of sales to artists)
- Consignment for sustainable designers
- Educational workshops on upcycling and sustainable living

## Target Customer

### Primary: Conscious Millennial Consumer
- **Age**: 25-35
- **Income**: $50K-$100K household
- **Location**: West Denver suburbs
- **Values**: Eco-conscious, creative, community-oriented, wellness-focused

### Shopping Behavior
- Prefers curated vintage boutiques over mass thrift
- Willing to pay more for unique, sustainable pieces
- Active on Instagram, values experiences

### Secondary: Curious Newcomers
- New to sustainable fashion
- Seeking accessible entry point
- Drawn by immersive environment

## Competitive Positioning

### The Gap
- West Denver is **underserved** by curated sustainable retail
- Competitors concentrated in RiNo, Capitol Hill, South Broadway
- No concept combines: immersive retail + cafe + community events

### Key Differentiators
1. Geographic white space (West Denver)
2. Integrated experience (clothing + cafe + events)
3. Authentic mission (giveback pledge)
4. Daily traffic driver (cafe)
5. Community moat (events, relationships)

## Growth Vision: El Woods Marketplace

Beyond the physical store, Year 2+ plans include an **online marketplace**:

- Etsy-like platform for thrifted, vintage, and eco-conscious goods
- Sell El Woods inventory to national audience
- Host other sustainable sellers and curators
- Platform revenue independent of physical location
- Scales brand without geographic constraints
